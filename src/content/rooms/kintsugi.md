Kintsugi is an old Kumaoni stone cottage that we restored. It's one room with a queen bed, a corner to sit in, and a bathroom with a skylight over the shower.

The room takes its name from the floor. Kintsugi is the Japanese way of mending broken pottery with gold, so the cracks become part of the piece. The floor here is painted like broken stone, with colour running through the cracks.

It stays warm in winter, with electric blankets and hot water through the day.
