Wabi and Sabi are two suites in one old stone house. Each has a bedroom and a small study with a day bed, so a third person can sleep there. Thick whitewashed walls, little niches in the stone, old fireplaces in the corners, and the same hand-painted floors as Kintsugi. The bathrooms have skylights too.

Wabi-sabi is the Japanese idea that things are beautiful because they are old, worn and a little uneven. We think the house agrees.

They're about 70 steps up the hill from the dining lounge, and look out over the garden.
