A small A-frame cabin that stands by itself under the trees. Downstairs is a sitting room with a wall of glass looking out at the valley. A steep wooden stair takes you up to the loft, where the bed sits right under the slope of the roof, with windows on both sides.

There's a place to sit outside under the trees, and the sunset is right in front of you. At night, look up through the skylights.
