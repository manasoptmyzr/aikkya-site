# Changing the website

Everything about www.aikkya.in lives in a folder on your Mac called **aikkya-site**. You don't need to open the files yourself. You tell Claude (or ChatGPT) what you want, it makes the change, shows it to you, and puts it live when you say so.

## With Claude

1. Open the Claude app and click **Code** at the top.
2. Pick the **aikkya-site** folder. The first time, choose it from your home folder. After that it's in the list.
3. Say what you want, in your own words. For example:
   - "Add a journal post about the first snow. The photos are in Downloads, the four from this morning."
   - "Make a journal post from this Instagram caption: …" (paste it, and say where the photos are)
   - "Kintsugi is now ₹10,000 a night."
   - "Add this review from Google, by Meera, March 2027: …"
   - "On the Us page, add that Billi is now properly ours."
4. It shows you the page in a preview on the right. Look it over and ask for changes if anything's off.
5. When you're happy, say **"put it live"**. It's on www.aikkya.in about two minutes later.
6. Changed your mind? Say **"undo the last change on the live site"**.

## With ChatGPT

Open the Codex app, choose the same **aikkya-site** folder, and ask the same way. Say "put it live" when you're done.

## Good to know

- Photos can be any size and straight off the phone. It shrinks them.
- If you paste your own words, it keeps them. Ask if you want them polished.
- It won't add a "Book now" button, pop-ups or trackers. That's on purpose, and it knows why.
- The first time, your Mac may ask whether Claude can open your Downloads folder. Say **Allow**. If Claude ever asks to run something, that's fine to allow too.
- There's a list of small things still to check in **TO-CONFIRM.md**. Say "go through TO-CONFIRM with me" when you have twenty minutes.
- Anything about passwords, GitHub, the domain or email: ask Manas.
